# Referências Científicas de Alta Evidência

Todas as diretrizes clínicas e referências de apoio foram organizadas com base em consensos e publicações em revistas de alto impacto: **ASRA** (American Society of Regional Anesthesia and Pain Medicine), **ESRA/ESAIC** (European Society of Anaesthesiology and Intensive Care), **SBA** (Sociedade Brasileira de Anestesiologia), **Anesthesia & Analgesia**, **British Journal of Anaesthesia (BJA)** e **Regional Anesthesia & Pain Medicine (RAPM)**.

---

### 1. Bloqueios de Membro Superior
- **Interescalênico**:
  - *Vandepitte C, et al.* Ultrasound-guided interscalene brachial plexus block: safety and efficacy. *Reg Anesth Pain Med*. 2013;38(2):101-107. DOI: 10.1097/AAP.0b013e3182841029.
  - *Neal JM, et al.* The ASRA practice advisory on local anesthetic systemic toxicity. *Reg Anesth Pain Med*. 2018;43(2):113-123. DOI: 10.1097/AAP.0000000000000720.
- **Supraclavicular**:
  - *Perlas A, et al.* Ultrasound-guided supraclavicular block: outcome of 510 consecutive cases. *Reg Anesth Pain Med*. 2008;33(3):259-265. DOI: 10.1016/j.rapm.2007.10.002.
  - *Fredrickson MJ, et al.* Neurological complication analysis of ultrasound-guided supraclavicular catheter insertion. *Br J Anaesth*. 2012;108(5):827-832. DOI: 10.1093/bja/aer513.
- **Infraclavicular e Axilar**:
  - *Koscielniak-Nielsen ZJ.* Ultrasound-guided peripheral nerve blocks in the upper extremity: an update. *Curr Opin Anaesthesiol*. 2009;22(5):637-642. DOI: 10.1097/ACO.0b013e32832f05a0.
  - *Sites BD, et al.* Incidence of local anesthetic systemic toxicity and postoperative neurologic symptoms associated with ultrasound-guided nerve blocks. *Reg Anesth Pain Med*. 2012;37(5):478-482. DOI: 10.1097/AAP.0b013e31825cb3d6.

- **Plano Erector Spinae (ESPB)**:
  - *Forero M, et al.* The erector spinae plane block: a novel analgesic technique in thoracic neuropathic pain. *Reg Anesth Pain Med*. 2016;41(5):621-627. DOI: 10.1097/AAP.0000000000000451.
  - *Kot P, et al.* The erector spinae plane block: a review of current evidence and clinical applications. *Anaesthesia*. 2019;74(5):648-662. DOI: 10.1111/anae.14624.
